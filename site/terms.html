Terms of service

These cover buying ranwhat Plus and using an account at account.ranwhat.com. The ranwhat tool itself is open source under the MIT licence, and that licence, not this page, covers using it.

Plus

What you buy, and how it ends.

In plain words, because a subscription you cannot understand is one you should not trust.

Who makes it Cenner, obrt za računalno programiranje i ostale usluge, vl. Matija Mikulinec, Mikulinci 8, Zagreb, Croatia. OIB 23152585818 · MB 99210363. Reachable at hello@ranwhat.com, and through the contact page.
Who takes payment Stripe sells you the subscription as merchant of record, through its Managed Payments service. Checkout, receipts and invoices come from Link, Stripe’s checkout, your bank statement shows LINK.COM*, and Stripe collects and pays any VAT or sales tax. Link’s support can help with a payment; anything about the feed itself comes to us.
What Plus is today A subscription for one organisation. It brings the capability catalogue feed at feed.ranwhat.com, which ranwhat update fetches with a token, so the scanner knows the scopes providers have added since your version shipped; and, on the organisation’s account at account.ranwhat.com, CI tokens for its pipelines and invites for its members, beside what every account has: a token for each machine linked with ranwhat login, and its billing. The features the pricing page marks as coming are not part of Plus until they ship. When each does, it comes to subscribers at no extra cost.
Price and billing €12 a month, or €120 a year, for one organisation however many machines, agents or people it has, including any VAT that applies. Checkout may show it in your own currency. Stripe takes payment at the start of each period and renews it automatically until you cancel. We will email you at least 30 days before a price change reaches you, so you can cancel first.
Your account Anyone may make a free account at account.ranwhat.com with an email address they hold. Keep that inbox secure, and any password, passkey or Google or GitHub account you add, because each of them signs in to your account. A free account costs nothing, needs no card, and can be closed at any time by writing to us from its address. Nothing that runs on your own machine needs one.
Organisations Every account starts with a personal organisation of its own, and can join others by invite. Plus is bought for an organisation, never for one person. An organisation’s owner answers for its members, its machines and its subscription, and its admins may invite and remove members, make CI tokens and manage billing. Members are unlimited at the one flat price above, and an organisation can send up to 20 invites a day. The owner can hand the organisation to one of its admins. Someone who leaves or is removed loses the terminals they linked to it; CI tokens stay with the organisation. A subscription stays with the organisation it was bought for and is never moved to another.
Cancelling Any time, with Manage billing on your account at account.ranwhat.com, on the billing page, in your Link account, or by writing to us. The subscription ends at the end of the period you have paid for, and the feed works until then. The organisation and its account stay, on Free, until you ask us to close the account. The rest of a period is not refunded, except as in the next row.
14-day refund Write to hello@ranwhat.com within 14 days of your first payment and we refund it in full, with no reason needed, and the subscription ends. This is the right of withdrawal EU law gives consumers, and we give it to businesses too. Renewals after that are not refunded, but you can cancel before the next one.
Your tokens Each machine linked with ranwhat login gets a token of its own, and each pipeline a CI token made on the account page, where an organisation can hold up to 50 CI tokens at a time; either can be revoked there, and a terminal’s token unused for 90 days is revoked by itself. A subscription bought before accounts keeps the token it was emailed, which works as before. Tokens are for your organisation’s own machines and CI. Keep them out of public repositories and do not share them outside the organisation. A token that turns up in public may be switched off, and we will give you a replacement. A machine’s or a CI token fetches the feed only while its organisation is on Plus, and an emailed token only while its subscription lasts.
Availability We aim to keep the feed up and make no promise of uptime. While it is down, the catalogue you last fetched, and the one bundled with the tool, keep working. If we ever stop running the feed, we refund the unused part of your period.
Liability ranwhat reports what it finds. It does not promise to find everything, and what you decide from its reports is yours to decide. As far as the law allows, our liability for anything to do with Plus is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the claim. Nothing here limits liability for intent or gross negligence, or any right a consumer has that a contract cannot take away.
Law Croatian law applies. If you are a consumer in the EU, you also keep the protection of the mandatory laws of the country you live in, and can take a claim to its courts. Write to us first: most things are settled by one email.
